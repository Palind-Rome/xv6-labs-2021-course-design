Q: Why are there missing keys with 2 threads, but not with 1 thread?
   Identify a sequence of events with 2 threads that can lead to a key being missing.
A: insert() updates a shared bucket head without synchronization. Suppose two different new
   entries e1 and e2 hash to the same bucket and both threads first read the old head h:

   thread 1: e1->next = h
   thread 2: e2->next = h
   thread 1: table[b] = e1
   thread 2: table[b] = e2

   The final assignment overwrites the head installed by thread 1. e1 is no longer reachable,
   so its key is missing. With one thread, these updates cannot interleave and no insertion is lost.
